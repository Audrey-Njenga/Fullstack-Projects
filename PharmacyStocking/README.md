# Pharmacy Inventory Intelligence & Optimisation System

## Overview
A data-driven system that helps hospitals optimise medicine inventory using three years of historical stocking, consumption, and procurement data.

## Problem
Hospital pharmacies often struggle with medicine shortages, overstocking, expiry-related losses, and inefficient use of inventory budgets. Traditional inventory systems provide limited insight into future demand, making procurement decisions heavily dependent on historical data and manual judgement.

## Solution
This system uses demand forecasting and inventory analytics to help hospitals make smarter procurement and replenishment decisions.

### Key Features
- **Demand Forecasting:** Predict future medicine requirements.
- **Stockout Detection:** Identify medicines at risk of running out.
- **Overstock Analysis:** Detect excess and slow-moving inventory.
- **Expiry Risk Monitoring:** Identify medicines likely to expire before use.
- **Replenishment Recommendations:** Suggest optimal order quantities and timing.
- **Financial Analytics:** Track inventory costs and identify potential savings.

## Objective
To ensure hospitals have the right medicines, in the right quantities, at the right time, while reducing shortages, wastage, and unnecessary inventory expenditure.
